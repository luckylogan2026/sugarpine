The problem

OMs are marketing.The truth is buried in the T-12.

Brokers inflate income. Expenses conveniently forget management, reserves, and the reassessment coming next January. Cap rates get computed off a pro-forma that doesn't exist yet. And every serious deal takes hours in a spreadsheet before you know if it's worth your time.

Farm it out instead, and a single professional underwrite runs hundreds to thousands of dollars, and days you don't have.

RVP Underwriter reads the documents the way a senior underwriter would. It normalizes the numbers, cross-checks the rent roll against the T-12, researches the local market live, and returns a verdict you can defend to a partner or a lender, in about a minute.

02 · Underwriting

Then it underwrites professionally

Normalized NOI, with receipts. Every adjustment shown line by line with the reasoning, so you can defend the figure to a partner or a lender.

Income never gets inflated. Rent roll and T-12 are cross-checked; stated occupancy has to earn its way into the math with evidence.

Live research on every deal. Today's debt market, county tax reassessment habits, insurance trends, market cap rates, local demand drivers. Researched fresh, not looked up in a stale table.

An expense benchmarker that flags padded lines and catches the missing ones: management, reserves, the usual suspects.

A second reviewer audits the work. An independent AI pass checks every adjustment before you see it, the way a senior underwriter checks an analyst.

Judgment calls are disclosed, not hidden. The decisions that moved the numbers, how confident it is in each, and which document would settle it.

Risks and red flags for THIS property. Researched for the specific park and county, ranked by severity. Not a generic checklist.

A plain-English deal brief up top, so you understand the story before you touch the spreadsheet.

03 · The offer

It tells you what to offer, and defends the number

Three ready offer structures. Conventional, bank plus seller second, and full seller carry. Each with the maximum price that still hits your targets, with DSCR and cash-on-cash shown.

It reads for seller-financing signals in the documents and structures around them when they are there.

A clear GO / NO-GO verdict with the recommended structure and why.

It never shrugs. Overpriced listings get the price that actually pencils. Unpriced listings get valued from income. You always leave with an offer you can send.

Stress tests before life runs them for you. Rate jumps, empty sites, tax reassessment, each with the DSCR it leaves behind.

A hidden-upside finder. Income the listing never mentions (utility bill-backs, below-market rents, ancillary revenue), quantified in annual NOI and value created.

Your targets, not ours. Set your own DSCR, cash-on-cash, and cost of capital. Every verdict and MAO honors them.

Talk to the underwriter. Ask why it made any adjustment, or tell it to change the numbers. "What if I get it at $2.2M with 20% down?" re-runs instantly.

A next-requests checklist. Exactly which documents to demand from the broker before you go further.

Questions

Fair questions.

How long does an underwrite actually take?

About a minute for the five headline numbers, and typically under three minutes for the full report, including the live market research pass.

What documents do I need to send?

Whatever you have. OM, T-12, rent roll, any subset works. The more you provide, the tighter the normalization. It even reads a zip.

Is this investment advice?

No. Everything the tool produces is a starting point for your own due diligence, not investment advice.

Where does the market data come from?

It's researched live, per deal, per county: debt market, tax reassessment habits, insurance trends, market cap rates. Not a stale table.

Why is your NOI different from the seller's?

Because it counts the costs the seller's books leave out: a management fee even if they self-manage, a capital reserve, realistic repairs, verified occupancy instead of the peak-season figure, and the property-tax bill you'd actually pay. Every adjusted line is shown with a plain-English reason, and an independent second AI pass audits the work before you see it. You verify rather than trust.

What happens to my deal data?

Your documents are processed to produce your analysis and are never shared or used for anything else. Saved deals live in your account, and everything sent to a lender or investor carries your identity, never ours.

Can I change the assumptions?

Yes. Set your own DSCR, cash-on-cash, and cost of capital. Ask the underwriter to re-run at a different price or leverage, and it re-runs instantly.
