Why commercial changes the math

In residential, you wait for the market to give you value. In commercial, you build it yourself.

In residential real estate, your property is worth what a comparable house sold for down the street. You have almost no control over that number. In commercial real estate, your property is valued as a multiple of its net operating income. Raise the income, and the value rises with it. Immediately, not after years of waiting for appreciation.

Before you understand this
  • ✗ You evaluate properties by price per unit or gut feel
  • ✗ You rely on the seller's stated income as the real number
  • ✗ You don't know what a fair price looks like until you've already paid it
  • ✗ Value creation feels abstract, something that just happens over time
After you understand this
  • ✓ You calculate a property's real value in under ten minutes
  • ✓ You normalize the seller's numbers to find what the asset actually earns
  • ✓ You know your target price before you talk to anyone
  • ✓ You can identify and quantify the upside before you close
