The fork in the road

There are two types of investors in this market. The difference between them is a process.

Both found the same deal. Both looked at the same listing. But only one of them actually knew what they were looking at.

Without a process

He found the deal on Crexi. The numbers looked interesting. He trusted what he was handed. He moved fast. He fell in love with the deal.

Months later, his lender stress-tested every assumption. Occupancy dropped in their model. He got a phone call he hadn't planned for, and suddenly needed to come up with six figures more at closing.

Not because it was a terrible deal. Because there was no cushion. Because he didn't build his own numbers first.

"I didn't know what I didn't know."
With a process

She found the same type of deal. She ran her own numbers. She normalized the income. She stress-tested the financing. She made an offer well below asking and justified every dollar of it with her own analysis.

The broker pushed back. She didn't flinch. They settled significantly below the asking price.

Six months later, she was looking for her next deal. The broker calls her first now.

"I knew my number before anyone else in the room did."
